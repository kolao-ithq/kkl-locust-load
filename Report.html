Up to 1,000 users (~600 RPS) performed well with low latency and zero failures. Beyond 1,000 users, failures climbed to 29% with P95 reaching 2.8s (spike to ~5s). Errors observed: HTTP 503 (Service Unavailable) and 524 (Gateway Timeout).

Key finding: Public UAT degrades significantly above 1,000 users due to gateway overhead (503/524 errors). Private internal handles ~10× more load before breaking, confirming the bottleneck is primarily at the public gateway layer, not the backend services alone.
Recommendation: For production peak draw, plan capacity based on Public UAT results (~1,000 concurrent users safe zone). Investigate gateway/CDN scaling and service replica counts before Lotlink integration testing.
